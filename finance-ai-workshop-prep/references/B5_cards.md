# B5 – Art-of-the-Possible Cards

Show two or three cards per process session (B1, block 4). Ask: "Would something like this help here? What would stop it working?" Plain language, no product promises; tier and tools stay in C1.

## Employee expenses and corporate cards

### Card feed and receipt capture set-up

| Today | With this in place |
| --- | --- |
| Receipts emailed, printed or lost; finance matches them to card lines by hand at month end. | Staff snap the receipt; it is matched to the card line automatically; finance sees only the lines still missing a receipt. |

*Human review:* Employee confirms the matched receipt; finance reviews unmatched lines only.  
*Library ref:* L-EXP-01

### Expense coding rules

| Today | With this in place |
| --- | --- |
| Employees pick from a long list of categories and finance fixes the coding at month end. | Most lines arrive coded correctly from rules; finance reviews the few with no rule. |

*Human review:* Finance reviews lines with no rule match.  
*Library ref:* L-EXP-02

### AI coding suggestion with exception review

| Today | With this in place |
| --- | --- |
| Finance works out the right code for each unusual line by reading the receipt and asking the employee. | Each unusual line arrives with a suggested code and the reason; finance approves or edits in one list. |

*Human review:* Finance approves every suggestion below the agreed confidence threshold; sample above it.  
*Library ref:* L-EXP-03
