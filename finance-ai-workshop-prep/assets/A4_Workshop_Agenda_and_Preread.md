## What the workshops are for

Over [N] sessions we will walk through how [process areas] work today, what slows the team down, and where automation and AI could help. You will get a short report within 5 working days of the last session. It sets out what we found, the options we recommend, and what it would take to put them in place.

We will favour options that use the systems you already have. A new tool or a custom build is only recommended where it clearly pays off.

## How each process session runs

1. We confirm what we learned from your questionnaire.
2. You show us one real transaction from start to finish, on screen.
3. We go through exceptions, checks and approvals.
4. We discuss what gets in the way, and ideas to fix it.
5. We check what would need to be true for a solution to work: access, licences, and who would own it.
6. We agree any follow-up questions and who will answer them.

## Recording and your data

- With your agreement, we record each session in Microsoft Teams and use the transcript to prepare accurate notes.
- Transcripts are processed only in tools approved by Deloitte ([Microsoft Copilot / Claude]). They are not used to train any AI model.
- Please don't show personal information on screen where you can avoid it (for example, filter out employee names).
- Files you send through the secure share link are kept for the engagement and handled under our standard confidentiality terms.
