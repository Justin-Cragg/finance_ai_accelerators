Draft a map with Claude

Claude sees this map (steps, lanes, connectors, details and open comments) and returns the whole map with your changes. Steps it keeps keep their comments and sub-processes. Every change is listed in a comment on the start, and Ctrl+Z undoes the update.

What you paste is sent to Claude on your account (approved tool under D3). Remove employee names, card numbers and other personal data first. The result is a draft: anything Claude inferred is added as a comment to confirm.

Export this map

The JSON file is what skill S2 reads to load the process into the C2 workbook. Mermaid and the image go into the report (Appendix B).

Shortcuts

  

    EnterAdd the next step after the selected one, same lane
    Shift+EnterAdd the next step in the lane below
    Ctrl+EnterAdd the next step in the lane above
    XAdd a decision (either/or) after the selected node
    A Add an AND split (all paths happen)
    OAdd an OR split (one or more paths)
    EAdd an end after the selected node
    JAdd a link to another process after the selected node
    SOpen the selected step's sub-process, or create one
    UGo up to the parent process, or back after following a link
    Draft with ClaudePaste a description, procedure or transcript and Claude drafts the map (hosted version only)
    F2Rename the selected node (or double-click it)
    M K !Toggle manual work, control, pain point
    CWrite a comment on the selected node
    DeleteDelete the selected node or connector
    Ctrl+ZUndo (Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo)
    LTidy the layout
    PPresentation mode on or off
    ArrowsMove the selection along the flow
  

  
Connect two nodes by dragging from the ● handle on the right of a node onto another node. Click a connector to label it (e.g. "Yes", "Over $500"). Drag the background to pan; scroll to zoom. Click the ⊞ marker on a step to open its sub-process, and the ↗ marker on a link to open the linked process.
