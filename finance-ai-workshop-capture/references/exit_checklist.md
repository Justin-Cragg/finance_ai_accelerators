# B4 – Session Exit Checklist

Use in the last 15 minutes of each process session. Every box must be ticked, or the gap logged as an open question with an owner and a due date before anyone leaves. These are the fields the build needs (Appendix H).

**Session:** [W#, process] · **Facilitator:** [ ] · **Scribe:** [ ]

## Solution fit (asked in every process session)

- [ ] **SOL-01** Which features of the tools you already pay for have you not switched on for this process, and why?  
  *Good answer:* Specific features, e.g. auto-categorisation, bank rules.
- [ ] **SOL-02** Who changes the configuration for this process today, and how long does a change take?  
  *Good answer:* A named role and a turnaround time.
- [ ] **SOL-03** If an automation removed most of this work, would you accept a new subscription? Up to what monthly cost?  
  *Good answer:* A yes or no and a spend range.
- [ ] **SOL-04** If this automation stopped working on a Monday morning, who would notice, and who would fix it?  
  *Good answer:* A named role and a fallback manual process.
- [ ] **SOL-05** Which steps must a person always review or approve, whatever the technology?  
  *Good answer:* Specific review points with thresholds.
- [ ] **SOL-06** Can we get test access to the systems and a set of real sample transactions to build and test against?  
  *Good answer:* Access route agreed with an owner and a date.

## Employee expenses and corporate cards

- [ ] **EXP-01** How many expense claims and card transactions do you process each month?  
  *Good answer:* Monthly counts for claims and card lines.
- [ ] **EXP-02** How many employees submit expenses or hold company cards, and does volume peak at any time of year?  
  *Good answer:* Counts of submitters and cardholders, with peak months.
- [ ] **EXP-03** Which expense tool do you use, and which plan or tier?  
  *Good answer:* Tool and plan name, e.g. Expensify Control.
- [ ] **EXP-04** Which card provider issues your company cards, and how do card transactions reach the expense tool?  
  *Good answer:* Card issuer and feed type with known failure frequency.
- [ ] **EXP-05** How do approved expenses get into the ERP: a native connector, a file export, or re-keying?  
  *Good answer:* The integration method and the ERP record type created.
- [ ] **EXP-06** What does your expense policy say about limits, receipts and non-allowable spend, and how is it enforced?  
  *Good answer:* A current written policy with limits by category.
- [ ] **EXP-07** Who approves expenses, at what limits, and what happens when an approver is away?  
  *Good answer:* An approval matrix with limits and delegates.
- [ ] **EXP-08** How are expenses coded: which dimensions (account, department, class, location, project or job, tax code), and who chooses them?  
  *Good answer:* Dimension list with who codes and the correction rate.
- [ ] **EXP-09** Is there a mapping from expense categories to GL accounts and tax codes, and can you share it?  
  *Good answer:* A current mapping table.
- [ ] **EXP-11** Walk us through one card transaction from purchase to being posted in the ERP, on screen.  
  *Good answer:* Each step with the person, system and time taken.
- [ ] **EXP-13** How do you reconcile the card statement to what was posted, and how often do they not match?  
  *Good answer:* A monthly reconciliation with a sign-off and a typical number of differences.
- [ ] **EXP-14** How and when are out-of-pocket claims reimbursed, and what is the month-end cut-off?  
  *Good answer:* A reimbursement route and cut-off date.
- [ ] **EXP-15** What goes wrong most often: duplicates, personal spend on company cards, wrong coding, late submission?  
  *Good answer:* A ranked list of exceptions with frequency.
- [ ] **EXP-16** Can you export one month of expense reports and card transactions, including the coding applied?  
  *Good answer:* An export file with coding and approval status.

## Bank statements into the ERP

- [ ] **BNK-01** How many bank accounts do you have, with which banks and currencies, and how many transactions per month on each?  
  *Good answer:* An account list with bank, currency and monthly lines.
- [ ] **BNK-02** How do bank statements get into the ERP today: direct feed, file download and upload, or manual entry?  
  *Good answer:* The method per account with frequency and time taken.
- [ ] **BNK-03** Does your bank or ERP offer a direct bank feed, and have you looked at enabling it?  
  *Good answer:* Feed availability per bank with any cost.
- [ ] **BNK-04** In what file formats can you download statements (CSV, OFX, QIF, CAMT.053, MT940), and do the formats differ by bank?  
  *Good answer:* A format per bank, with samples.
- [ ] **BNK-05** Walk us through importing a statement and reconciling it, on screen.  
  *Good answer:* Each step with the person, system and time taken.
- [ ] **BNK-06** How are bank lines matched to ERP transactions today, and which matching rules are set up?  
  *Good answer:* A list of current rules and the auto-match rate.
- [ ] **BNK-07** Which items are hardest to match (e.g. batched receipts, card settlements, fees, foreign exchange), and how do you resolve them?  
  *Good answer:* Item types with monthly counts and effort.
- [ ] **BNK-08** How do customer receipts get applied to invoices: by bank reference, remittance email or manual lookup?  
  *Good answer:* The method with the share of receipts needing manual lookup.
- [ ] **BNK-09** Who prepares and who reviews bank reconciliations, how often, and how is sign-off evidenced?  
  *Good answer:* Preparer, reviewer, frequency and evidence of sign-off.
- [ ] **BNK-11** Can you share three months of statements in their native format, plus the matching ERP report for the same period?  
  *Good answer:* Native statement files and an ERP export for the same months.
- [ ] **BNK-12** Who can grant access to the bank portal and the ERP bank import features, and is two-factor authentication tied to a person?  
  *Good answer:* An access route that does not depend on one person's phone.

## Cash flow forecasting

- [ ] **CFF-02** What horizon and granularity does the forecast cover (e.g. 13 weeks weekly, 12 months monthly), and how often is it refreshed?  
  *Good answer:* Horizon, buckets and refresh frequency.
- [ ] **CFF-03** Where is the forecast built today, and who owns the model?  
  *Good answer:* Tool and owner, with the model file.
- [ ] **CFF-04** Which inputs feed the forecast: AR ageing, AP ageing, payroll, tax, capex, debt, sales orders, pipeline?  
  *Good answer:* Each input with its source system and how it is pulled.
- [ ] **CFF-05** For each input, how is the data pulled into the model: report export, copy-paste or typed?  
  *Good answer:* A method and time per input.
- [ ] **CFF-06** Which assumptions drive the forecast (e.g. debtor days, payment runs, seasonality), and how are they set and reviewed?  
  *Good answer:* Documented drivers with an owner.
- [ ] **CFF-07** Walk us through one forecast refresh from start to finished output, on screen.  
  *Good answer:* Each step with the person, system and time taken.
- [ ] **CFF-12** Can you share the current forecast model and the last three months of forecast versus actual?  
  *Good answer:* The model file and variance history.

## Generic process (any process without its own module; also the starting point for writing a new module)

- [ ] **GEN-01** How many items does this process handle each month, and what counts as one item (e.g. an invoice, a journal, a reconciliation)?  
  *Good answer:* A monthly count with its unit.
- [ ] **GEN-02** Does volume peak at certain times (month end, year end, season)?  
  *Good answer:* Peak months with rough volumes.
- [ ] **GEN-04** Which systems, spreadsheets and mailboxes does this process use?  
  *Good answer:* Every system and file touched.
- [ ] **GEN-05** How does data move between those systems: integration, file upload, re-keying or email?  
  *Good answer:* A method per hand-off.
- [ ] **GEN-06** What starts the process, and what marks it as finished?  
  *Good answer:* A clear start trigger and end point.
- [ ] **GEN-07** Walk us through one real, recent item from start to finish, on screen.  
  *Good answer:* Each step with the person, system and time taken.
- [ ] **GEN-08** Which rules decide how items are coded, matched, approved or routed?  
  *Good answer:* Documented rules with an owner.
- [ ] **GEN-09** Who reviews and approves the work, at what limits, and how is sign-off evidenced?  
  *Good answer:* Reviewer, limits and evidence of sign-off.
- [ ] **GEN-10** What goes wrong most often, how is it found, and how long does it take to fix?  
  *Good answer:* A ranked list of exceptions with frequency and effort.
- [ ] **GEN-13** Can you export one month of the items this process handles, with the outcome of each (coded, matched, approved)?  
  *Good answer:* An export with outcomes.
- [ ] **GEN-14** Are there procedures, checklists or policies for this process you can share?  
  *Good answer:* Current written procedures.
